- Goblin Ambience mod (analysed 2026-09-08, `Downloads/Goblin_Ambience_GN1.37_GNX1.3.11.7_ALL_3_`) — candidate to fold into GNX core. Two parts: (1) a normal GNX data mod `zz_goblin_ambience` with sounds.json declaring 12 voice_banks named `<species>_<kind>` (species goblin/hobgoblin/ogre, kind ambient/breeding/victory/defeat) + 33 CC0 .ogg clips (~336KB); (2) a data.win patch replacing s_sfx.gml / obj_control_Step_0.gml / s_raid_function.gml with the playback engine (~430 lines of scr_gnx_goblin_* functions). Engine: per-frame `scr_gnx_goblin_ambient_step()` picks visible on-camera obj_mon of mon_type 0/1/3 (tentacle=2 silent) and plays a positional clip via audio_play_sound_at; `scr_gnx_play_goblin_raid_result(win)` on raid end weights victory/defeat by front_row/back_row troop counts. 3 scheduling variants differ ONLY in scr_gnx_goblin_ambient_step + a few helpers: A balanced per-species timers (gob 3-7s/hob 4.5-9s/ogre 6.5-12s), B three generic lanes (~3-7s, README-recommended), C per-instance gnx_voice_next var 3-6s. Compat with 1.38: `global.gnx_voice_bank_names` exists; `scr_gnx_generate_voice` DOES random-pick from it so ambience banks would leak into captive moans — mod works around this with a per-frame `scr_gnx_goblin_hide_voice_banks()` that filters names starting `zz_goblin_ambience.`. raid_function win/loss hook points are structurally identical to 1.37. Cleaner integration: filter ambience banks out of `gnx_voice_bank_names` at load time by naming convention instead of per-frame. <!-- id:5a8a -->
